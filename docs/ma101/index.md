# MA101: Discrete Mathematics & Mathematical Notation

Welcome to **MA101: Discrete Mathematics & Mathematical Notation**!

When reading computational biology papers, machine learning algorithms, or algorithm specifications, you will encounter a dense language of symbols: $\sum$, $\prod$, $\in$, $\forall$, $\cup$, $\setminus$, and $\mathbb{R}$.

The goal of this course is **not** to work through abstract theorem-proving, but to serve as a friendly, practical **decoder and introduction to the formal language of mathematics**.

---

## Course Modules

1. [**Summation ($\sum$) & Product ($\prod$) Notation**](sums-and-products.html)
   * *Rationale*: How to read, expand, manipulate, and nest Greek summation and product symbols without getting lost in indices.
2. [**Sets, Number Systems & Set-Builder Notation**](sets-and-logic.html)
   * *Rationale*: Understanding standard number sets ($\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}$), set-builder conditions, set operations ($\cup, \cap, \bigcup, \bigcap, \setminus, \emptyset, A^c$), and their mapping to programmatic data sets.
3. [**The Pigeonhole Principle & Discrete Counting**](pigeonhole-principle.html)
   * *Rationale*: A deceptively simple mathematical principle that powers hash collision proofs, exact string matching ($k$-mer filtering in sequence alignment), and combinatorial limits.
